What Happens in
Body Doubling

Virtual coworking for Gen X women with ADHD · No agenda · No pressure

Body doubling is an ADHD intervention where the presence of another person
helps your brain stay regulated and on task. We work alongside each other.
The Claude AI tip is a bonus.

1
0–10 minutes

Check In & Set Intentions

Your name, where you are, and what you're working on today. One sentence is enough. No need to explain it.

2
10–50 minutes

Silent Cowork

Everyone muted, cameras on. Soft music. Claude open in a tab. Work on whatever you brought. No performance required.

3
50–60 minutes

Share Out

What did you work on? Did you use Claude? Share one thing — even if it's just "I finally opened the doc."

4
After session

Drop It in Circle

Post what you got done in the community. Even one email counts. Especially one email. We celebrate all of it.

✦

Claude AI Tip — Each Session

Each session includes a 2-minute Claude tip in the share-out. Session 1: the brain dump. Type everything overwhelming you into Claude, then ask: "Organize this into what needs action today, what can wait, and what's not actually my problem."
