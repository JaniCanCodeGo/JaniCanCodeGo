Concentration

A two-minute brain break. Flip two cards at a time and find all the matching pairs — fewest moves wins. Built as a "Before Apps" workgroup project.

Moves: 0 Matches: 0 / 6 Best (fewest moves): —
Cleared it! That focus you just used? Same muscle you lead with.

How to play

  • Click or tap a card to flip it over — or Tab to it and press Enter.
  • Flip a second card. If the two match, they stay face up.
  • If they don't match, they flip back — remember where they were.
  • Clear the whole board in as few moves as you can.

Want to build this yourself? This game is a project in the Build With Claude workgroup's "Before Apps" module.
