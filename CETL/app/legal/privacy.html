Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

Clear Enough To Lead ("CETL," "we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through the CETL website, podcast, and Build With Claude workgroup (together, the "Services"), and how we use it. The short version: this site collects as close to nothing as we can manage.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

The website itself has no accounts, forms, or sign-ups. If you enroll in the workgroup or contact us directly (for example, by email), we receive the information you choose to share, such as your name and email address.

Information stored on your device

The site stores a small amount of data in your browser's local storage, which never leaves your device and is never sent to us:

  • Theme preference — whether you chose the light or dark theme;
  • Game scores — your personal best in the Concentration game.

You can clear this at any time through your browser's settings.

Information collected automatically

This site does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or third-party cookies. If the site is hosted by a third-party hosting provider, that provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested) for security and operations under its own privacy policy.

2. How we use information

We use information you provide only to run the workgroup, respond to you, and send communications you asked for. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their marketing.

3. Podcast platforms

When you listen to the podcast through a third-party app or directory (such as Apple Podcasts or Spotify), that platform collects listening data under its own privacy policy. We may receive only aggregate, non-identifying statistics from those platforms.

4. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, including rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). Because we collect so little, exercising these rights is usually as simple as emailing us and asking. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any privacy right.

5. Children

The Services are intended for adults and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data security and retention

We keep the information you share with us only as long as needed for the purposes above, and we take reasonable measures to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the current version. Material changes will be noted on this page.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach out through the contact information provided on the podcast's directory listings or the workgroup's enrollment communications.
