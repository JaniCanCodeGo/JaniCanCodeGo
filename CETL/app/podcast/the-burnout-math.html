Episode 3: The Burnout Math

Why doing 40% today and 100% tomorrow beats doing 120% today and nothing for three days — and how to actually believe that.

About this episode

Run the numbers: the sprint-and-crash cycle loses to steady, honest pacing every single week — and yet the sprint keeps winning, because it feels like commitment. This episode is about doing the burnout math out loud, catching the moment you're about to spend three days of energy on one afternoon, and building the kind of pace you can lead from for years.

Show notes

  • The actual arithmetic of sprint-and-crash versus steady pace
  • Why 120% days feel productive and what they really cost
  • Energy budgeting for ADHD brains that love a deadline rush
  • How to hold a sustainable pace without feeling like you're slacking
