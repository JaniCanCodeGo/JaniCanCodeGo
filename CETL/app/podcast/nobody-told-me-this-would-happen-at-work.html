Episode 2: Nobody Told Me This Would Happen At Work

Perimenopause symptoms don't check your calendar. A real conversation about showing up to lead through them anyway.

About this episode

The hot flash in the middle of the budget presentation. The word that vanishes mid-sentence in a meeting you're running. The night of no sleep before the biggest day of your quarter. Nobody put any of this in the leadership books, so we're talking about it here — plainly, without shame, and with actual strategies.

This is the episode to share with the colleague who thinks she's the only one.

Show notes

  • The symptoms nobody warns you will show up in a conference room
  • Scripts for the moments words fail you — literally
  • What to disclose at work, to whom, and when (and when not to)
  • Building a workday that bends instead of breaking
