Episode 1: The Bar Is Clear Enough

What "good enough leadership" actually looks like when you're managing a team, your own brain, and your own body all at the same time.

Audio coming soon. This episode is in production — the player will appear here when it's published.

About this episode

Somewhere along the way, "having it together" became the price of admission for leadership. This episode takes that idea apart. We talk about what your team actually needs from you (hint: it's clarity, not perfection), why the bar you hold yourself to is probably calibrated for a person with a different brain and a different body, and how to reset it to something you can clear every day — even the foggy ones.

If you've ever ended a workday convinced you failed everyone because you weren't operating at 110%, this one is for you.

Show notes

  • Why "good enough leadership" is a standard, not a compromise
  • The difference between what your team needs and what your inner critic demands
  • Recalibrating the bar for a brain with ADHD and a body in perimenopause
  • One question to ask yourself at the end of every workday
