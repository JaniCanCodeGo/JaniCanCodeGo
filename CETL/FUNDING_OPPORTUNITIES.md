# CETL — Funding Opportunities Research

**Prepared:** August 2026 · Companion to the Business Plan (v2.0) and Portfolio
**Target:** $25,000 non-dilutive (per Business Plan §8.4)

> **How to read this document.** Every opportunity is labeled by verification level:
> **[Verified]** = confirmed against primary sources with independent cross-checking;
> **[Sourced]** = consistent across multiple current sources but confirm details on the
> program's own site before applying (several grant sites block automated verification);
> **[Strategy]** = an approach, not a program. Deadlines shift — always re-check the
> linked page. CETL's profile for fit purposes: US woman-owned, pre-revenue, solo
> founder, education/community business, not venture-track.

## 1. Best near-term fits (pre-revenue friendly)

### Kiva U.S. — 0% interest microloan [Sourced]
- **Amount:** $1,000–$15,000, **0% interest, no fees**, 12–36 month repayment
- **Eligibility:** no credit-score minimum, no revenue requirement, no collateral —
  explicitly open to brand-new businesses
- **How it works:** character-based underwriting; a 15-day private period where your own
  network funds first, then Kiva's ~2M-lender crowd funds the rest
- **Why it fits CETL:** the private-funding phase doubles as an audience-building test —
  exactly the muscle Phase 0 needs — and a $10–15K Kiva loan alone covers most of the
  §8.4 budget
- **Source:** https://www.kiva.org/borrow ; review coverage: https://www.cnbc.com/select/kiva-small-business-loan-review/

### Amber Grant (WomensNet) [Sourced]
- **Amount:** $10,000 monthly awards (WomensNet states $30K+ awarded monthly across
  categories); year-end awards on top for monthly winners
- **Eligibility:** women 18+ owning ≥50% of a US/Canada business; all categories; one
  application enters all applicable grants; small application fee applies
- **Cadence:** monthly — a low-effort, repeatable shot on goal
- **Why it fits CETL:** startup-friendly, no revenue minimum, and the CETL story
  (midlife women building tech skills) is strong essay material
- **Source:** https://ambergrantsforwomen.com/get-an-amber-grant/ ; guide: https://ambrook.com/funding/amber-grant-for-women

### HerRise MicroGrant (HerSuiteSpot / Yva Jourdan Foundation) [Sourced]
- **Amount:** $1,000 monthly
- **Eligibility:** US businesses ≥51% woman-owned, under $1M revenue, focus on
  under-resourced women entrepreneurs and women of color; $15 admin fee; two narrative
  questions — no business plan or financials required
- **Cadence:** monthly, apply by month-end
- **Source:** https://www.hersuitespot.com/herrise/

### IFundWomen Universal Grant Application [Sourced]
- **How it works:** one application enters a standing database; when corporate partners
  (Visa She's Next, American Express, Caress, adidas, and others — $170M deployed to
  date) launch a grant, matching businesses are notified automatically
- **Cost/effort:** free; one form; passive after submission
- **Why it fits CETL:** zero-maintenance pipeline into women's-grant programs as they
  open
- **Source:** https://www.ifundwomen.com/grants/universal-grant-application-0 ; https://ifundwomen.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360049631034

### SBA Microloan Program [Sourced]
- **Amount:** up to $50,000; average loan ≈ $13,000; rates generally 8–13%; max 7-year
  term; administered by nonprofit community intermediary lenders (CDFIs) that also
  provide technical assistance
- **Why it fits CETL:** sized exactly for a $25K ask; intermediaries routinely lend to
  startups a bank would decline — and pair the loan with free advising
- **Source:** https://www.sba.gov/funding-programs/loans/microloans ; https://www.paychex.com/articles/finance/sba-microloan

### SBA Women's Business Centers — free advising, not cash [Verified]
- The national network of **138 WBCs remains fully funded in FY26** (the July 2026 $6M
  Modernization competition funds WBC host nonprofits, not businesses — CETL cannot
  apply to it directly, and no action is needed)
- **Use:** free one-on-one counseling and low-cost training — specifically loan
  packaging for the SBA microloan above and grant-application readiness
- **Source:** https://www.sba.gov/article/2026/07/21/sba-announces-6-million-competition-womens-business-center-modernization

## 2. Rolling corporate/platform programs

### Hello Alice grant marketplace [Sourced]
- Standing marketplace of small-business grants and "Boost Camp" accelerator+grant
  programs (recent cycles included a $10K–$100K Small Business Growth Fund and a
  BOTOX® Cosmetic Confidence Collective with twenty $20K awards for women entrepreneurs);
  programs recur — create a profile and watch the women-owned filters
- **Source:** https://helloalice.com/funding/grants/ ; https://app.helloalice.com/grants

### Google Cloud Free Program [Verified]
- **$300 in credits** (90-day validity; billing account required) plus 20+ always-free
  products, no application — verified live in August 2026
- **Use for CETL:** hosting cohort web projects and agent demos at zero cost; note the
  Google for Startups larger credit tiers ($200K–$350K) require VC backing and are
  **not** a fit
- **Source:** https://cloud.google.com/free ; https://cloud.google.com/startup

## 3. Podcast-specific funding

- **AIR Media "New Voices"** — professional development + $1,000 stipend for
  early-career audio creators from underrepresented backgrounds; applications
  historically open each May. [Sourced] https://www.thepodcasthost.com/business-of-podcasting/podcasting-grants-uk-usa/
- **Monthly micro-grants for podcasters** (trailer/pilot funding up to ~£1,000,
  prioritizing underrepresented voices) and women-of-color podcaster micro-grants —
  small but stackable. [Sourced] https://rephonic.com/blog/podcast-grants/ ;
  https://www.descript.com/blog/article/podcast-grants-101
- Reality check: the era of big platform funds (Spotify Sound Up etc.) has contracted;
  treat podcast grants as garnish, not the meal.

## 5. The funding CETL controls outright [Strategy]

**Presell the beta cohort.** Twelve beta seats at $299 is **$3,588 of non-dilutive,
non-repayable funding** that also validates demand — the strongest possible exhibit in
every grant application above. Sequence matters: presale receipts + Kiva loan first,
grant applications (which take months) running in parallel.

## 6. Recommended 90-day action sequence

| When | Action | Expected value |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | Submit IFundWomen Universal Application (free, one-time) | Passive grant pipeline |
| Week 1 | Book free advising at nearest SBA Women's Business Center | Loan packaging + grant readiness |
| Week 2 | First Amber Grant application; re-enter monthly | $10K shots on goal |
| Week 2 | HerRise application if eligibility fits; re-enter monthly | $1K shots on goal |
| Month 1–2 | Kiva application + 15-day private funding round | $10–15K at 0% |
| Month 2–3 | Hello Alice profile + apply to open women-owned programs | $5K–$20K cyclical |
| Month 3+ | Presell beta seats to the email list | ~$3.6K + demand proof |
| Year 2–3 | Tory Burch Fellows (once revenue ≥ $75K) | $5K + network |

**Honest bottom line:** none of these is guaranteed money. The reliable path to the $25K
in Business Plan §8.4 is a stack: Kiva ($10–15K) + beta presales (~$3.6K) + one or two
grant wins from the monthly programs ($1K–$10K) + the free-resource layer (WBC advising,
Google Cloud credits) reducing what needs funding at all.
