# Feedback to Site: Missing Information Letter Template

Tone rules: courteous, appreciative, district-ready. Missing data is a
completeness issue, never a finding and never an accusation of
noncompliance. No em-dashes anywhere. Arial font in the generated .docx.
Authored edits are attributed to Murjani McTier, never Claude.

Output files per Step 1 of the agent workflow:
- `Missing_Information_Report_[School].md` (internal working report)
- `Feedback_to_Site_[School].docx` (this letter, for the district)

[Date]

[Principal or Site Coordinator Name]
[School Name]
[District Name]

RE: Facilities Review Guide, Additional Information Needed, Civil Rights
Review [CRR 20 (2025-26) or CRR 21 (2024-25)]

Dear [Name]:

Thank you for completing and returning the Facilities Review Guide for
[School Name]. We appreciate the time and care your team put into
documenting the site's facilities.

During our review of the completed guide, we identified a few items where
additional information is needed before the review can be finalized. These
items are listed below by section. Please note that this request reflects
only the completeness of the submitted guide; it is not a finding and does
not indicate noncompliance.

**Items needing completion:**

[For each item, one bullet:]
- [Section name], [field name]: [what is missing, stated neutrally. For
  example: "The Year Built field is blank. This date is needed to
  determine which accessibility standard applies to this area."]

[Standard categories to check, from Step 1:]
- Blank measurement fields
- Entries marked "Unknown"
- Unanswered Yes/No questions
- Missing Year Built or ADA Modification Date (required to determine the
  applicable standard)
- Contradictory entries (identify both entries and ask which is correct)
- Missing signature block
- Skipped conditional follow-ups (for example, a ramp reported at an
  entrance but the Ramps section not completed for it)

Please return the updated information by [date, typically 10 business
days]. You may send only the corrected pages; a full resubmission is not
necessary. If any item is unclear, or if a measurement cannot be obtained,
please contact me and we will work through it together.

Thank you again for your partnership in this review.

Sincerely,
