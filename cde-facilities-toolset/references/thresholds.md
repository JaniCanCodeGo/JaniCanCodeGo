# Compliance Thresholds by Standard

Single source of truth for the Guide's printed notations, the HTML form's
auto-compliance logic, and the LOF agent's Step 3 analysis.

> VERIFY EACH CYCLE against the current instrument and the U.S. Access
> Board technical guides before reuse. Values at exactly a threshold
> boundary are COMPLIANT.
>
> Program Access areas have NO measurable thresholds: observational review
> plus the 14 staff interview questions only. Never apply this table to a
> Program Access element.

Where standards differ, every variant is listed. Where only one value is
shown, the standards are aligned for CRR purposes. "Reach" means the
generic operable-part rule: max 48 inches forward reach; side reach max
54 inches under pre-2010 standards and max 48 inches under 2010 ADA.

## 1. Accessible Parking

| Measurement | ANSI / UFAS / 1991 ADA | 2010 ADA |
|---|---|---|
| Accessible space width | Min 96 in | Min 96 in |
| Access aisle width | Min 60 in standard; van min 96 in | Min 60 in (van space may be 132 in with 60 in aisle) |
| Van space width | Min 96 in plus 96 in aisle | Min 132 in, OR 96 in with 60 in aisle |
| Vertical clearance, van route | Min 98 in | Min 98 in |
| Space and aisle surface slope | Max 1:48 in all directions | Max 1:48 |
| Count | Per table (1 per 25 up to 100 total spaces; verify per cycle) | 2010 ADA Table 208.2 |

## 2. Accessible Routes / Walkways

- Clear width: min 36 in.
- Passing space: 60 in by 60 in at max 200 ft intervals when route is
  under 60 in wide (2010 ADA).
- Overhead clearance: min 80 in.
- Protruding objects: max 4 in projection when leading edge is between
  27 in and 80 in above finish floor.
- Running slope over 1:20 makes the route a ramp: apply Ramps section.
- Cross slope: max 1:48.

## 3. Stairways and Steps

- Stair width: min 44 in for public stairs.
- Riser height: max 7 in. Tread depth: min 11 in. Nosing: no abrupt
  underside; contrast strip recommended (verify instrument wording).
- Handrail height: 30 to 34 in under ANSI / UFAS; 34 to 38 in under
  1991 ADA and 2010 ADA.

## 4. Ramps

- Clear width between handrails: min 36 in.
- Running slope: max 1:12. Cross slope: max 1:48.
- Max rise per run: 30 in before an intermediate landing.
- Landings (top, bottom, intermediate, direction change): min 60 in by
  60 in.
- Handrail height: 30 to 34 in under ANSI / UFAS; 34 to 38 in under
  1991 ADA and 2010 ADA. Required both sides when rise exceeds 6 in.
- Edge protection required (curb, barrier, or extended surface).

## 5. Curb Ramps

- Width of ramp run, not counting flared sides: min 36 in.
- Running slope: max 1:12. Cross slope: max 1:48. Flared sides: max 1:10.
- Top landing: min 36 in by 36 in.
- Detectable warnings where required (verify per cycle).

## 6. Entrances, Doors, and Gates

| Measurement | ANSI | UFAS / 1991 ADA / 2010 ADA |
|---|---|---|
| Clear doorway width | Min 32 in | Min 32 in |
| Door handle height | Max 48 in | Max 48 in |
| Opening force, interior hinged | Max 8.5 lbs | Max 5 lbs |
| Threshold height, exterior and interior | Max 1/2 in | Max 1/2 in |
| Hardware | Operable with one hand, no tight grasping, pinching, or twisting of the wrist | Same |

## 7. Rooms, Offices, and Administration

- Signage: raised characters and braille, 60 in to centerline
  (2010 ADA: baseline between 48 in and 60 in; record 57 to 63 in
  centerline band per instrument, verify per cycle).
- Doorway, handle, force, threshold: same as area 6.
- Reception or service countertop: max 34 in under ANSI / UFAS; max 36 in
  under 1991 ADA / 2010 ADA (with min 36 in wide lowered section).
- Knee clearance at work surfaces: min 27 in high, 30 in wide, 19 in deep.
- Forward reach to operable parts: max 48 in.

## 8. Elevators / Lifts

- Call button height: 15 to 48 in to panel center. Button size: min
  3/4 in diameter (2010 ADA).
- Floor designation characters: min 2 in, raised and braille.
- Entrance and car door width: min 32 in.
- Car interior: min 51 in wide by 51 in deep (center door) or 68 in wide
  by 51 in deep (side door), 2010 ADA.
- Emergency communications: max 48 in to bottom of panel.
- Controls: 15 to 48 in to panel center. Door reopening device required,
  no physical contact (verify sensitivity rule per cycle).
- Platform lifts: end doors and side doors min 32 in; platform min 32 in
  wide by 48 in deep.

## 9. Accessible Drinking Fountains

- Spout outlet height: max 36 in.
- Water flow: min 4 in high arc.
- Knee and toe clearance beneath: min 27 in high.
- Hi-lo pairs: 50 percent low, 50 percent standing height (2010 ADA 211).
- Water coolers and bottle fillers: highest operable part max 48 in.

## 10. Cafeteria (including service areas)

- Doorway, handle, force, threshold: same as area 6.
- Fixed tables and counters: 28 to 34 in; wheelchair space 30 in by
  48 in; knee clearance 27 in / 30 in / 19 in.
- Food service line aisle: min 36 in clear.
- Food line counter or tray slide: max 34 in under ANSI / UFAS; max 36 in
  under 1991 ADA / 2010 ADA.
- Self-service items, tableware, condiments: max 48 in forward reach.
- Checkout: min 1 accessible aisle, min 36 in wide; counter max 34 in
  (ANSI / UFAS) or max 36 in (1991 / 2010); lowered section min 36 in wide.
- Tray return slot: max 48 in; clear floor space 30 in by 48 in.
- Beverage stations and vending: highest operable part max 48 in; clear
  floor space 30 in by 48 in.

## 11. Library

- Doorway, handle, force, threshold: same as area 6.
- Aisles: min 36 in; 60 in passing per 2010 ADA where required.
- Turning space in reading areas: min 60 in diameter.
- Tables and workstations: 28 to 34 in; knee clearance 27 / 30 / 19 in.
- Checkout counter: max 34 in (ANSI / UFAS) or max 36 in (1991 / 2010);
  lowered section min 36 in wide.
- Book drop: slot max 48 in forward reach (side reach max 54 in pre-2010,
  max 48 in under 2010 ADA); clear floor space 30 in by 48 in; operable
  force max 5 lbs; operable without tight grasping, pinching, or wrist
  twisting; on an accessible route.
- Stacks: aisle min 36 in; overhead clearance min 80 in.
- Card catalog: aisle min 36 in; operable parts max 48 in forward reach.

## 12. CTE Classrooms and 13. CTE Labs and Shops

- Doorway, handle, force, threshold: same as area 6.
- Fixed tables, counters, workstations: 28 to 34 in.
- Aisles: min 36 in.
- Knee clearance: 27 / 30 / 19 in.
- Soap and paper towel dispensers at classroom sinks: max 48 in.

## 14. Gymnasium / Auditorium / Weight Room / Other Assembly

- Wheelchair spaces: count per 2010 ADA Table 221.2.1.1 by seating
  capacity; dispersed, with companion seats and comparable lines of sight.
- Wheelchair space dimensions: min 36 in wide by 48 in deep (front or
  rear entry; 60 in deep for side entry, verify per cycle).
- Assistive listening: system type and receiver count per 2010 ADA 219
  (receivers scale with capacity, verify per cycle).
- Ramps to stages or seating: apply Ramps section.

## 15. Stadium / Field

- Same assembly rules as area 14, plus accessible route to the field,
  team or player seating access, and accessible toilet facilities serving
  the venue.

## 16. Dressing, Fitting, and Locker Rooms

- Entrance doorway min 32 in; turning space min 60 in diameter.
- Accessible lockers: min 5 percent of each type (2010 ADA 225); operable
  parts max 48 in forward reach.
- Mirror: max 40 in to bottom of reflecting edge.
- Benches: top 17 to 19 in; depth 20 to 24 in; clear floor space 30 in by
  48 in adjacent; back support or against wall.
- Transfer shower: curb max 1/2 in; grab bars 33 to 36 in to centerline;
  seat 17 to 19 in; handheld hose min 59 in (2010 ADA).
- Male / Female / Non-Gender Specific designation recorded per instance.

## 17. Restrooms

- Entrance: min 32 in; turning space min 60 in diameter or T-turn.
- Lavatory: rim max 34 in; knee clearance min 27 in high, 30 in wide,
  19 in deep; pipes insulated or shielded.
- Dispensers, dryers, coat hooks: max 48 in. Mirror: max 40 in to
  reflecting edge.
- Accessible stall: min 60 in wide by 56 in deep; door min 32 in.
- Grab bars: 33 to 36 in to centerline; side min 40 in long (42 in per
  2010 ADA 604.5.1, verify per cycle); rear min 36 in long.
- Water closet seat: 17 to 19 in. Flush controls: max 44 in, on the open
  side.
- Urinal: rim max 17 in; clear space min 30 in wide by 48 in deep.
- Male / Female / Non-Gender Specific designation recorded per instance
  (California requires Non-Gender Specific restrooms).

## 18. Public Telephones

- If none provided, mark N/A.
- Clear floor space: min 30 in by 48 in.
- Cord length: min 29 in.
- TTY keypad touch surface: max 48 in.
- Volume control and hearing aid compatibility (verify per cycle).

## Restored sections beyond the fixed 18 LOF rows

These appear in the Guide and HTML form; findings roll up into the
closest LOF row or are reported narratively (verify placement per cycle):

- Passenger loading zones: access aisle 60 in by 20 ft, vertical
  clearance 114 in at the zone.
- Consolidated signage: raised characters, braille, mounting height and
  location rules.
- Swimming pools / aquatic (2010 ADA 242): pool lift or sloped entry per
  pool size.
- Play areas (2010 ADA 240): ground-level and elevated play component
  ratios, accessible surfacing.
- Accessible means of egress / areas of rescue assistance.
- Medical and first-aid areas.
- Portable / temporary classrooms: ramp, landing, door hardware.
- Parking lot lighting and surface condition (observational).
