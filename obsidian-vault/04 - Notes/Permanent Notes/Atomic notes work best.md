# Atomic notes work best

One idea per note. When a note holds a single, clearly-titled idea, it's
easy to link, easy to find, and easy to reuse later in writing.

This is the core idea behind the Zettelkasten method — and it's what makes
the graph view (and Smart Connections) genuinely useful.
