# CRR 2: Annual Public Notification (APN)

> Sources: CDE OEO / Randi Solís Thompson, 2026-05-12
> Raw: [CRR 2 APN Directive](../../raw/civil-rights-review/2026-05-12-crr-2-apn-directive.md)

## Overview

The Annual Public Notification (APN) is a standalone, once-a-year requirement under
CRR 2. CDE's Office of Equal Opportunity has clarified that it does not have to be
reprinted on every CTE-related flyer, publication, or document. A CRR 2 finding is
warranted only when a school provided no APN at all.

## When a Finding Applies

A finding is appropriate only when the school provided **nothing** for the APN:

- No website publication
- No printed notice
- No notification of any kind

If the school published the APN once through any reasonable channel, the standalone
annual requirement is met.

## When a Finding Does NOT Apply

Do not issue a CRR 2 finding solely because the APN was missing from individual CTE
materials. The absence of the notice on a specific flyer, brochure, or course
document is not, by itself, a violation. Reviewers should look for whether the APN
exists somewhere as an annual notice, not whether it is repeated across every piece
of CTE collateral.

## Reviewer Guidance

This is guidance for reviewers writing the Summary of Findings. The CRR processing
agent does not enforce it in code; the corrective actions in the Voluntary
Compliance Plan reflect exactly what the reviewer wrote. Reviewers should confirm a
true absence before writing a CRR 2 corrective action.
