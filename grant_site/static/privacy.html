Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 7, 2026. This policy is posted conspicuously in the site footer as required by the California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA, Bus. & Prof. Code §§ 22575–22579).

Summary — privacy by design

  • We do not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers, or fingerprinting.
  • We do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA, and never have.
  • Run data is processed in memory and generated documents are stored only on the server that hosts your instance; you can delete a run at any time.
  • We never ask for, and you should never enter, Social Security numbers or ITINs — the IRS collects those directly when you submit Form SS-4.

We do not collect precise geolocation, biometric information, or sensitive personal information categories under Civ. Code § 1798.140(ae), and we do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 (see the Children’s Privacy & CIPA page).

Third parties (CalOPPA § 22575(b)(1), (6))

To perform lookups you request, the agent contacts: your organization’s own website; the ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer API (EIN search); and USPTO services (trademark status). Only the search terms you provide (organization name, state, proposed mark) are transmitted. No third party is permitted to collect personally identifiable information about your online activities over time and across sites through this service, and we do not respond differently to users based on such collection.

Do Not Track (CalOPPA § 22575(b)(5), (7))

Because we do not track users across sites or over time, this site behaves identically whether or not your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal — no tracking occurs either way. We honor the Global Privacy Control by design.

Your California rights (CCPA/CPRA, Civ. Code § 1798.100 et seq.)

  • Right to know/access: every piece of data used in a run is included in the downloadable Agent Reports file.
  • Right to delete: use the delete-run API (DELETE /api/runs/{id}) or contact the site operator; run data is not retained after deletion.
  • Right to correct: re-run the agent with corrected inputs; nothing else is stored.
  • Right to opt out of sale/sharing: not applicable — we do not sell or share personal information. Accordingly no “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information” link is required, but requests are honored as confirmations.
  • Non-discrimination: exercising your rights never changes the service you receive.

Submit requests to the operator of your instance. We respond within 45 days as required by § 1798.130. Authorized agents may submit requests with written permission.

Federal notice

This service is not a consumer reporting agency (FCRA) and not a financial institution (GLBA). Grant, EIN, and trademark documents you generate are your records. Where the operator is a federal agency or contractor, Privacy Act of 1974 notices are provided at the point of collection.

Data retention and security

Generated documents remain on the hosting server until you delete the run. Transport should be protected with HTTPS in production deployments. We minimize collection: no accounts, no passwords, no payment data.

Changes and contact

Material changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date before they take effect (CalOPPA § 22575(b)(3)). Contact: the operator listed for your deployment.
