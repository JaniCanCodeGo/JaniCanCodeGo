Children’s Privacy, COPPA, and CIPA Statement

Audience

Grant Writing Studio is a business tool intended for adults acting on behalf of organizations. It is not directed to children under 13.

COPPA (15 U.S.C. §§ 6501–6506; 16 C.F.R. Part 312)

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
  • No accounts, profiles, chat, or user-generated public content exist on this site.
  • If we learn that a child under 13 has submitted personal information, we will delete it promptly. Contact the site operator to report this.

California minors (Bus. & Prof. Code § 22581 — “Online Eraser” law)

Registered users under 18 have the right to request removal of content they posted. This site has no registration and no posting, so no such content exists; any removal request will nonetheless be honored and confirmed. We do not market to minors any of the products or services listed in § 22580(i) — no advertising of any kind is served.

Children’s Internet Protection Act (CIPA, 47 U.S.C. § 254(h)) — school and library deployments

CIPA places obligations on schools and libraries that receive E-Rate discounts (Internet safety policies, technology protection measures/filtering, and monitoring). While CIPA regulates those institutions rather than individual websites, this site is designed to be safely usable in CIPA-compliant environments:

  • No content harmful to minors: the site contains no obscene content, no child sexual abuse material, and no material harmful to minors as defined in 47 U.S.C. § 254(h)(7)(G); nothing on the site should trigger technology protection measures.
  • Filter-friendly: the site functions without third-party embeds, pop-ups, chat rooms, direct messaging, or social features (relevant to Internet-safety-policy requirements around minors’ use of email/chat and unauthorized disclosure of minors’ personal information).
  • No collection from minors: the service collects organizational business data from adult users only, supporting schools’ obligations to prevent unauthorized disclosure of minors’ personal information.
  • No circumvention tools: the site provides no proxying, anonymizing, or filter-bypass functionality.
  • For deployments operated by a school or library: the operating institution remains responsible for its own CIPA Internet safety policy, filtering, public notice, and hearing requirements, and for E-Rate certifications (FCC Form 486/479).

Student data (FERPA / SOPIPA note)

This tool processes organizational and business information, not student education records. Do not enter student personal information into any field. If a school entity uses this tool, that practice keeps it outside FERPA-protected records and SOPIPA-covered student profiles (Bus. & Prof. Code § 22584).
