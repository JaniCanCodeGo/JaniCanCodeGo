📏 State SOQ Rules of Thumb

• Follow the posting's instructions exactly — font, page limit, and order are scored.
• Default convention: Arial 12 pt, 1-inch margins, your name and JC number on every page.
• Restate each factor as a heading, then answer in the same order.
• Use the STAR pattern: Situation → Task → Action → Result, with numbers.
• Address every part of a multi-part factor; don't just repeat your resume.
