# Clear Enough To Lead — Low Energy Morning Check
*Template: low energy / all symptoms / overachiever burnout pattern / podcast in progress*

---

**First 5 minutes — body first, everything else can wait:**

1. **Water before anything else.** Cold if you're hot, room temp if the nausea is talking. Non-negotiable.
2. **Eat something small and bland.** Crackers, toast, a banana. Nausea lies — empty stomach makes it worse.
3. **Temperature check.** Hot flash here or incoming? Fan on, layer off, cold cloth on the neck. Handle it now before it derails your first meeting.
4. **Sit up slowly.** You already know why.

---

**Brain online — barely, but enough:**

5. **Your ONE anchor task today.** One. Not three "quick ones" that somehow become eleven. Write it on a sticky note. That's it. Step away from the list.
6. **Open your Outlook calendar, nothing else.** See what's actually required today before email rewrites your entire agenda.
7. **Your grace item:** One thing that can wait until tomorrow. Already decided. Not up for debate — especially not with yourself.

---

**Overachiever tax — read this:**

8. **You are not behind.** Low energy days are not wasted days. Burning through your reserves today means borrowing from tomorrow, and tomorrow's version of you will have opinions about that.
9. **The burnout math:** Doing 40% today and 100% tomorrow beats doing 120% today and 0% for the next three days. You've seen this movie. You know how it ends.

---

**Typo acknowledgment — because it's happening today:**

10. **Before you send anything:** Read it once. Just once — not seventeen times, that's perfectionism pretending to be proofreading. If it makes sense and isn't offensive, it goes. A typo in an email does not cancel your credentials. You have built things, managed people, and held it together on days that would've broken someone else. A missing "the" is not your legacy.

---

**Podcast corner — KungFuJay is still happening, just not all today:**

11. **One podcast micro-task only.** Not recording, not editing, not building the whole content calendar. Pick ONE: jot a topic idea, write one sentence about what you want to say, or voice-memo a thought before it disappears into the fog. That counts. That IS the work on a low day.
12. **Your story is still there.** Brain fog does not erase what you know. The ADHD, the perimenopause, the managing while barely managing — that's the content. The messy days are not obstacles to the podcast. They ARE the podcast.

---

**Before you open email:**

*"Clear enough to lead today means I showed up, I did the one thing, and I didn't set myself on fire trying to prove something to people who are eating lunch during my presentations anyway."*
